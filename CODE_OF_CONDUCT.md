# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

Expected behavior:

- be considerate and assume good faith;
- accept constructive feedback and give it kindly;
- focus disagreement on ideas, code, and trade-offs rather than on people;
- respect that maintainers' time is finite and volunteered.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory
language, publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption of
discussions or reviews.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages, and code review — and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or
permanently ban anyone whose participation harms the community.

Report concerns privately to `conduct@tinyhumans.ai`. Reports are handled
confidentially, and the reporter's identity is not shared with the reported
person. Do not use public issues to report conduct concerns.
